# Official competition masthead artwork

The negative assets are exact, unmodified copies downloaded from the official sources on 2026-09-25. They are used only in the competition mastheads; the existing positive navigation assets remain unchanged.

- Premier League negative logo: https://logo.premierleague.com/img/lion-light.svg
- Premier League brand guidance and RGB 55, 0, 60 (#37003C): https://logo.premierleague.com/
- © Copyright The Football Association Premier League Limited, 2016.
- LALIGA negative horizontal wordmark: https://assets.laliga.com/assets/logos/LALIGA_RGB_h_monocromatico_negativo/LALIGA_RGB_h_monocromatico_negativo.png
- LALIGA official downloads: https://www.laliga.com/en-PT/pressroom/logos-and-corporate-dossier/logos
- LALIGA official digital coral: #FF4B44, declared as --lp-colors-competition-primera-division-normal and --lp-colors-reds-red550 in the stylesheet served by https://www.laliga.com/en-PT (https://www.laliga.com/_next/static/css/0d6657649d74a8f5.css). The official website's LALIGA wordmark also uses this exact fill.

No generic Pantone-to-HEX conversion was used. Hover, borders and control backgrounds are CSS colour mixes derived from each official digital colour; they are interface tones, not additional official brand colours. Liga Portugal keeps its existing artwork and blue palette.

## LALIGA complete negative masthead presentation (2026-09-26)

`laliga-lockup-negative.svg` presents the official white LL symbol alongside the official white LALIGA wordmark. The white vector paths were extracted from the PDFs in the following official download packages, without redrawing or distorting either mark:

- Symbol: https://assets.laliga.com/assets/logos/LL_RGB_v_monocromatic_negativo/LL_RGB_v_monocromatic_negativo.zip
- Wordmark: https://assets.laliga.com/assets/logos/LALIGA_RGB_h_monocromatico_negativo/LALIGA_RGB_h_monocromatico_negativo.zip

The black PDF preview backgrounds and exterior margins are omitted. The two marks are arranged in one transparent SVG for the existing masthead image box; this combined presentation is local, while both marks retain their official outlines. The original negative PNG and positive navigation artwork remain unchanged.

La Liga masthead text uses charcoal. Its control, active and hover backgrounds keep a darkened coral mix, reduced to 8% black so that the charcoal labels retain readable contrast.
